What can I get help with?

If you belong to a Scheduled Caste and want to start or grow a business, study, or buy equipment, this service helps you find the right low-interest government loan, know what you will repay every quarter, and reach the nearest office that can help you.

First, choose your state

Loan schemes, interest rates and offices are different in every state. Your state decides what we can show you.

Three things this service does

Good to know before you start

This service only guides you. It does not give loans or collect applications.

Schemes differ by state

The loan amount, interest rate and repayment period of a scheme can be different from one state to another.

Partners differ too

Each partner office follows its own limits. We show only offices that currently have funds and a good repayment record.

Your details stay with you

What you enter here is used only to show you options. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved without your action.

The numbers shown are estimates to help you plan. The final amount, interest rate and repayment plan are confirmed by the partner office when you apply.
